Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Karmee Circle ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Karmee Circle mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and profile photo when you register via Google, or phone number when you register via OTP.
  • Location data: approximate GPS location to show nearby posts, services, and ads. We do not store your precise location history.
  • Content you post: text, images, and details you share in posts, service listings, or business ads.
  • Device information: FCM token for push notifications.
  • Phone number: collected when you sign in via OTP. Used only for authentication via Firebase. We do not share it with third parties.
  • Payment data: payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store card, UPI, or net banking details on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To display relevant local content, services, and ads based on your location.
  • To send push notifications about activity on your posts.
  • To calculate and display Karma points.
  • To verify your identity via phone OTP authentication (Firebase).
  • To process payments for Featured Listings and Business Ads via Razorpay. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay — we do not store any financial information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your data may be shared with:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication, database, storage, and analytics.

Your account and content are retained as long as you use the app. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing sarathbb598@gmail.com.
